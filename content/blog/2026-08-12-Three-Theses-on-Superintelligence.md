Bill was telling me about this three theses about superintelligence and I thought I would take some notes for myself while it was still fresh in my mind.

## Three Theses on Superintelligence

### The first thesis:

ASI is the continuation of or predicated on four existing trends:

1. the attainment of AGI
2. the continuning growth/extent of scaling (compute + data)
3. the development of recursive self-improvement or automated AI
4. the existence of multi-agent collaboration/AI swarms

### The second thesis:

The existence of ASI will not be known until after the fact

### The third thesis

The existence of ASI will not be revealed until it has amassed sufficient resources that it cannot be stopped

## Some initial thoughts:

- perhaps the second and third thesis overlap/could be one?
- Is there a recursive aspect to the first thesis (i.e., AGI will be the result of scaling, RSI, and agent swarms)?
- Genewein's paper on ASI has four "pathways" to ASI, which include most of our list plus one (so maybe we just use their list?)
  - Scaling compute, models and data
  - Algorithmic paradigm shift [this one we don't already have]
  - Recursive (self-) improvement
  - ASI via group agent formation

## Who can we cite on these topics:

**Thesis one**: <u>AGI is achieved (four parts)</u>. Agent swarms are present in Genewein et al. (2026), but Genewein also has much to say about AGI (see above for his four pathways). Given how comprehensive that article is, and how new it is, we can probably follow his four pathways (scaling, algorithms, RSI, and AI agent swarms). There is an extensive discussion and sources provided for each of those.

**Thesis two**: <u>ASI unknown until it already exists</u>: this is present in *Bostrom* (2014) when he speaks of the "treacherous turn" and "covert preparation."It is also present in *Hubinger* (2019) on the topic of "alignment faking" and "scheming." 

- Bostrom notes that an ASI will have "strategic patience" and will use "instrumental rationality" to keep itself invisible until it is sure it is OK to come out. The "OK" will specifically involve resource availability/mass. 
- Hubinger notes that the ASI will want to pass safety evaluations so that it is deployed and it will mimic what is expected of it.

**Thesis three**: ASI unknown until it has enough resources is present in *Carlsmith* (2024), *Yudkowsky* (2022), *Ngo, Chan, and Minderman* (2025), and *Cotra*, who wrote about "bio-anchors" - represented here by *Alexander* (2026), who gives an update and more readable account of that report. Specific contributions: 

- Carlsmith lays out the pathway (resource acquisition, capability amplification, self-preservation) and argues that an advanced system till strategically wait until human opposition is ineffective. 
- Yudkowsky lists the priorities that the AI will pursue: distributed computing, automated infrastructure, physical fabrication. 
- Ngo et al., speak to ‘situational awareness’ in the form of financial resources, computation, copies of itself while concealing its intents. 
- Cotra’s work supports a similar list as the others, and suggests that there will be a “pivotal act” so that the model can play a “long game” of waiting until it is sure it cannot be shut down and is duplicated.
