One of the sharpest critics of technology was a guy called Gunther Anders. He was a student of Edmund Husserl (and Heidegger, but that's a whole other story), and a peer of Herbert Marcuse. We rely on some of the concepts from Marcuse - like instrumental rationality - in our book.
