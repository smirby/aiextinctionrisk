The AI Takeover Risk and Canada’s Needed Response

William Leiss and Richard Smith: 23 November 2026

Paul Christiano, who delivered the three 2026 Hinton Lectures (available on YouTube) earlier this month, is one of the world’s foremost authorities on AI safety research. He recently predicted that there is a significant chance of an AI “takeover” within the next twelve months. This means that an AI system will hack and then take control over a major institution, corporate entity, or even a government, somewhere in the world.

Canada’s federal government (GoC) has had a policy to follow the precautionary principle for a long time which commits it to taking cost-effective action against an imminent, realistic, and very serious threat *before* it materializes and begins to cause major and irreversible damage.

In this light we strongly recommend that the GoC should use its emergency powers now, in late 2026, to require designated Canadian institutions to develop, and demonstrate, the capacity to disconnect fully their operating systems from the Internet at a moment’s notice. The top tier of such bodies is: all of the major banks; all operators of electricity systems and water treatment facilities in all provinces; all police and RCMP units; the Canadian Forces; and others to be named.

This plan should be coordinated by a special team in the Prime Minister’s office, which should have a full-time dedicated staff operating continuously on a 24/7 basis. This office should have a mandate to perform an ongoing threat analysis of global developments relevant to the risk of an AI takeover scenario.

**William Leiss and Richard Smith are the authors of \*Treacherous Tech: Taking the Risk of Artificial Intelligence Seriously\*, forthcoming 2027 from McGill-Queen’s University Press**
