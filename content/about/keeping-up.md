### Prominent Canadian AI Researchers & Pioneers 

- [Geoffrey Hinton](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F02jndp&q=Geoffrey+Hinton): Known as one of the "Godfathers of AI," his foundational work in deep neural networks at the University of Toronto launched the modern AI revolution. [[1](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DG4TySBNDAv/)]
- [Yoshua Bengio](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F0c3fzxy&q=Yoshua+Bengio): Founder of [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en) in Montreal and a recipient of the Turing Award, he is a world leader in deep learning and AI safety/alignment research. [[1](https://macleans.ca/society/the-power-list-ai/), [2](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/advisory-council-artificial-intelligence/en/member-biographies-advisory-council-artificial-intelligence), [3](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DG4TySBNDAv/)]
- [Richard Sutton](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fm%2F02qz8pp&q=Richard+Sutton): A professor at the University of Alberta and Amii, he pioneered reinforcement learning principles that underpin modern AI feedback systems like ChatGPT. [[1](https://www.ualberta.ca/en/research/our-research/artificial-intelligence.html), [2](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DG4TySBNDAv/)]
- [Andrej Karpathy](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11f8bqvtw4&q=Andrej+Karpathy): A Slovak-Canadian researcher known for his work in deep learning and computer vision at OpenAI, Tesla, and Anthropic, plus founding Eureka Labs. [[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrej_Karpathy)]
- [Ilya Sutskever](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11c0tb7z_k&q=Ilya+Sutskever): A prominent researcher and former Chief Scientist at OpenAI who shaped large-scale neural network breakthroughs.
- [Aidan Gomez](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11f0xz7hk2&q=Aidan+Gomez) & [Nick Frosst](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11fyyhps8k&q=Nick+Frosst): Co-founders of Canadian AI unicorn [Cohere](https://cohere.com/), advancing enterprise large language models.

Full list from Wikipedia: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:Canadian_artificial_intelligence_researchers
